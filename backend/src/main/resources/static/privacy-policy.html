TruCaller Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

1. Introduction

TruCaller ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TruCaller mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

By using TruCaller, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Full name - provided during registration
  • Phone number - used as your primary identifier (E.164 format)
  • Email address (optional) - for account recovery
  • Password - stored securely using bcrypt hashing; we never store plaintext passwords

2.2 Device Information

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifier (Android ID)
  • Device status (active, stolen, inactive, flagged)
  • Registration timestamp

2.3 Contact Information

  • Contact names and phone numbers from your device's address book
  • Contact email addresses (if available)
Why we collect contacts: Your contacts are synced to enable caller identification, spam detection, and secure cloud backup. Contact data is associated only with your account and is not shared with other users without your knowledge.

2.4 Network and Location Data

  • IP address
  • ISP (Internet Service Provider)
  • Approximate location (city, country, latitude/longitude derived from IP)
  • Network type (Wi-Fi or mobile data)

2.5 Caller ID Data

  • Phone numbers reported or looked up
  • Spam scores and categories (safe, suspected spam, spam, fraud)
  • Report counts

2.6 Blocked Numbers

  • Phone numbers you choose to block
  • Reason for blocking (if provided)

2.7 Stolen Device Reports

  • Report details and description
  • Last known IP address and location
  • PIN verification status
  • Alarm and remote action logs (remote alarm, location request, device lock)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Caller Identification: Identifying incoming callers and detecting spam/fraud numbers
  • Anti-Theft Protection: Tracking stolen devices, triggering remote alarms, locking devices, and generating stolen device reports
  • Contact Backup: Securely backing up your contacts to the cloud for recovery
  • Account Management: Authentication, login, and account security
  • Service Improvement: Improving spam detection accuracy and app functionality
  • Security Monitoring: Logging IP addresses and device information to detect unauthorized access

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on secure servers using MongoDB with encrypted connections
  • Passwords are hashed using the bcrypt algorithm and are never stored in plaintext
  • Authentication is handled via JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with configurable secrets
  • All API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • We implement access controls to ensure only authorized personnel can access user data

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Caller ID Database: Phone numbers and associated names contribute to our shared caller identification database to help all users identify callers
  • Law Enforcement: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests, particularly for stolen device reports
  • Service Providers: We use third-party services (e.g., IP geolocation via ip-api.com) that may process limited data to provide functionality

6. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For phone authentication and cloud services
  • Google Drive: For optional data backup and sync
  • ip-api.com: For IP-based geolocation services

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing its use of your data.

7. Device Administrator Permissions

TruCaller may request Device Administrator privileges on your Android device. This permission is used exclusively for anti-theft protection features including:

  • Remote device locking
  • Remote alarm triggering
  • Device location tracking when reported stolen

You may revoke Device Administrator access at any time through your device settings.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke your consent to data collection at any time by discontinuing use of the App
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

IP logs and alarm logs may be retained for up to 12 months for security and auditing purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

TruCaller is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy within the App. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
