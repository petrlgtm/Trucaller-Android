TruCaller is a powerful caller identification and anti-theft protection app built for Android. Know who is calling before you answer, block spam and fraud numbers, and keep your device safe with advanced security features.

KEY FEATURES

Caller Identification
- Identify unknown callers using a community-driven database
- See spam scores and fraud warnings before answering
- Community-verified spam reports for higher accuracy

Spam & Call Blocking
- Block unwanted calls and SMS messages
- Automatic spam detection with configurable blocking schedules
- Bulk report spam numbers to protect the community

Contact Management
- Sync and back up your contacts to secure cloud storage
- Restore contacts when switching devices
- Smart contact search and organization

Anti-Theft Protection
- Report your device as stolen and track its last known location
- Trigger a remote alarm on your missing device
- Remote device lock via Device Administrator
- IP and network logging for forensic recovery

Family & Group Safety
- Create family groups to monitor devices together
- Send alerts to family members
- Shared device tracking for peace of mind

Call Recording
- Record incoming and outgoing calls
- Manage and play back recordings from the app
- Configurable recording settings

Analytics Dashboard
- View your call and spam statistics
- Track blocking effectiveness over time
- Community contribution metrics

Privacy & Security
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt; never stored in plaintext
- JWT-based authentication with secure token management
- All API communications encrypted over HTTPS
- Full account deletion available at any time

TruCaller is made with care in Uganda. Your privacy matters to us -- we never sell your data to third parties.
